## Content

Les Vagues by ELIE SAAB is the premier branded residential development on Qetaifan Island North in Lusail, Qatar.
- Design: Elegantly envisioned by haute couture designer Elie Saab, featuring flowing architectural lines reminiscent of ocean waves.
- Offerings: 1, 2, and 3-bedroom sea-front luxury apartments with floor-to-ceiling glass windows and panoramic Gulf views.
- Amenities: Private beach access, landscaped swimming pools, high-end fitness center, and branded fashion boutiques.
