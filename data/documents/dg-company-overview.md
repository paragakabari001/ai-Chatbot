## Content

Dar Global PLC is an international luxury real estate developer listed on the London Stock Exchange (LSE: DAR). It was established to pioneer luxury second-home development across prime global destinations, combining high-end design, exclusive branded hospitality, and world-renowned fashion icons.

Core mission: Discover DarGlobal: Your Gateway to Luxury Real Estate Investment

Key Pillars:
- Global Footprint: London (UK), Dubai (UAE), Muscat (Oman), Doha (Qatar), Costa del Sol / Marbella (Spain), Bosnia, and Maldives.
- Co-branding with global luxury icons: ELIE SAAB, Automobili Lamborghini, Pagani Automobili, Missoni, Trump Organization, W Hotels, Aston Martin, and Mouawad.
- Target Audience: High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs) seeking capital appreciation, prime rental yields, and residency/golden visa opportunities.
- Governance: Adheres to the high corporate governance and transparency standards of the London Stock Exchange.
