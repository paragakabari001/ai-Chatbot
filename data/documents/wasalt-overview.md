## Content

Wasalt (wasalt.sa) is Saudi Arabia's leading real estate platform and digital marketplace.

Overview: Wasalt is a Saudi-based real estate platform that makes it easier to discover properties, explore opportunities, and connect with property experts across the Kingdom. Whether you’re looking to buy, rent, sell, or invest, Wasalt brings the right properties, insights, and services together in one place.

Key Platform Capabilities:
- Verified Properties: 100% compliant with Saudi Real Estate General Authority (REGA / Fal license) standards to prevent fraudulent listings.
- Residential & Commercial: Comprehensive listings for rent, buy, and lease across all 13 Saudi provinces.
- 360-degree Virtual Tours: High-resolution virtual walkthroughs and floor plans.
- Interactive Map Search: Precise GIS-backed map exploration of neighborhoods, nearby mosques, schools, healthcare centers, and metro stations.
- Real Estate Auctions (Mazadat): Direct access to judicial, governmental, and private real estate auctions across the Kingdom.
- Off-Plan Developments: Showcase of major Wafi-licensed residential and commercial master communities.
