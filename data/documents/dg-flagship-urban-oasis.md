## Content

Urban Oasis is a vibrant luxury residential tower located directly on the Dubai Water Canal, boasting interiors curated by the renowned Italian fashion house Missoni.
- Design aesthetic: Signature Missoni zig-zag patterns, artistic geometries, and vibrant color palettes throughout common areas and living spaces.
- Offerings: Studios, 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom canal-facing residences.
- Amenities: Infinity swimming pool overlooking the canal, state-of-the-art gymnasium, private aquatic lounges, and 24/7 concierge.
