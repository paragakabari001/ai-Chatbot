## Content

Wasalt operates in strict accordance with the Saudi Ministry of Housing and Real Estate General Authority (REGA):
1. Fal License Verification: Under Saudi law, all property advertisements must have a valid Fal license number issued by REGA. Wasalt verifies licenses to protect buyers and tenants from fictitious ads.
2. Ejar System Integration: In Saudi Arabia, all residential and commercial tenancy contracts must be registered electronically on the government 'Ejar' network to be legally binding and executable in court.
3. Wafi Program Compliance: Off-plan sales must be licensed under the Wafi program to guarantee escrow account protections for purchasers.
4. Transparent Commission: Brokerage commission is legally capped at 2.5% of the transaction value unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing.
