## Content

Dar Global implements a structured, transparent unit reservation mechanism:
1. Token Amount: Customers may secure an exclusive reservation on a selected unit by paying a non-refundable token amount.
2. Reservation Window: The reservation period lasts strictly seven (7) calendar days from the date the token amount is received.
3. Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA): Before the 7-day reservation period expires, the customer must:
   a) Pay the initial required deposit installment of the purchase price in full.
   b) Sign and execute the Developer's official Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA).
4. Forfeiture Condition: If the buyer fails to complete the deposit payment or sign the SPA within the 7-day window, the reservation automatically terminates and the token amount is forfeited without further notice.
5. Payment Gateways: Supports international wire transfers and secure card payments via Tap Payment Gateway.
