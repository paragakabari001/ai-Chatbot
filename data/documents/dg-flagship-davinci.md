## Content

DaVinci Tower is an ultra-exclusive residential landmark on the Dubai Water Canal in Downtown Dubai, featuring interiors crafted by Italian hypercar legend Pagani Automobili.
- Design: Inspired by Horacio Pagani's philosophy of combining art and science, with exposed carbon fiber accents, bespoke Italian furnishings, and kinetic architectural elements.
- Residences: 2 to 4 bedroom apartments and signature penthouses with sweeping views of the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Water Canal.
- Developer: Dar Global PLC.
