## Content

Wasalt organizes real estate into well-defined categories tailored to Saudi market needs:
1. Residential Properties for Rent & Sale:
   - Apartments (Shuqqa): Studios, 1-3 bedroom standard and duplex apartments in residential buildings.
   - Villas (Fella): Standalone villas, duplexes, and townhouses with private courtyards, maid's rooms, and swimming pools.
   - Floor Units (Dor): Full independent residential floor in a villa, very popular among Saudi families.
   - Gated Compounds: Expatriate and executive compounds offering 24/7 security, clubhouses, and recreational facilities.
2. Commercial Real Estate:
   - Offices (Makatib): Fitted, semi-fitted, or shell-and-core corporate office spaces.
   - Retail Shops & Showrooms: Commercial strip malls and street-front shops.
   - Warehouses & Industrial: Storage and logistics facilities in designated municipal zones.
3. Land Plots (Aradi):
   - Residential land plots ready for construction.
   - Commercial plots suitable for mixed-use developments and retail plazas.
