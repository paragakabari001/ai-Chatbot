## Content

Tierra Viva is an ultra-luxury residential enclave situated in Benahavís, near Marbella on Spain's Costa del Sol, developed in collaboration with Automobili Lamborghini.
- Residences: 53 private villas perched across multiple elevation tiers overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.
- Villa Types: Diamante (6 bedrooms), Zafiro (5 bedrooms), and Esmeralda (4 bedrooms).
- Features: Direct elevator access to all levels, rooftop terraces, private infinity pools, manicured gardens, and design inspired by Lamborghini sports cars.
