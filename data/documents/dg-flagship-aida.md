## Content

AIDA is a master-planned 3.5 million square meter mixed-use cliffside oceanfront community developed by Dar Global in Muscat, Oman, perched 130 meters above the Gulf of Oman.
- Partnerships: Features the Trump International Golf Club Oman, an 18-hole championship golf course, alongside a luxury Trump Hotel and villas.
- Offerings: 3 to 7 bedroom luxury villas, ocean-view townhouses, and golf-front residences.
- Ownership: Freehold for all nationalities (Integrated Tourism Complex status), granting permanent residency eligibility in Oman.
- Location: Just 20 minutes from downtown Muscat and Muscat International Airport.
